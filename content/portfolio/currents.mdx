Currents is a small weekend project that became a small month project. It pulls live swell, wind, and tide data from a handful of NOAA buoys and renders them as slowly-shifting ribbons. There's no notifications, no alerts, no streaks. It just sits on a second monitor.

## Stack

- **Svelte 5** for the runtime, because reactivity for live data is the one thing Svelte still does best.
- **D3** for the line/area rendering, but only the parts I couldn't do with SVG paths by hand.
- **Cloudflare Workers** for the proxy, since some buoy feeds are flaky and I didn't want to write a real backend.

## Why

I built it because the surf cams I actually trusted were all blogs that hadn't updated since 2014. I wanted something that felt like looking at a window.
