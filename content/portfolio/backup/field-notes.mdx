Field Notes is the project that finally taught me to stop pitching and start shipping. I had been talking about it for two years before I sat down and built the first version in a weekend.

## The problem

Every group trip I've ever been on has a "the spreadsheet" — a Google Doc with tabs, half-finished links, a list of dates that nobody has confirmed. The spreadsheet works until it doesn't, usually around the time someone books a non-refundable flight.

## What I tried

The first version was a kanban board. People hated it — it felt like work. The second version was a chat. People loved it but couldn't find anything. **Field Notes is the third version:** a chat that writes itself into a plan, with the spreadsheet always one tap away.
