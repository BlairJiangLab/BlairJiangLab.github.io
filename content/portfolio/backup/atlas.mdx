Atlas started as a Figma library and ended as a way of working. Over fourteen months we rebuilt the visual language of a mid-sized fintech, but the harder problem was getting forty engineers, six PMs, and a rotating cast of contractors to actually use it.

## What shipped

- **Three-tier token system** — primitive → semantic → component, with Figma variables synced to CSS custom properties.
- **48 components**, all dark-mode aware, all keyboard-navigable.
- **Adoption kit** — migrations scripts, lint rules, and a one-page "how we design" guide pinned in the team wiki.

## What I learned

The first version of Atlas was beautiful and unused. The second version was uglier and ubiquitous. **Adoption is a UX problem.** The library that wins is the one that's easier to copy-paste than to roll your own.
